# Report April 2026

Design Team: @iogy | @Mine

## Preparation for Zcon Vozes2

During the month of May, our team focused its efforts on the visual and creative preparation for Zcon Vozes2. We developed a meticulous design process, aligning visual identity, graphic materials, communication pieces, and event support elements. This process involved layout studies, color selection, visual organization of information, and the creation of materials that strengthen the project's presence.

All the design work was conceived to convey professionalism, clarity, and connection with the Zcon Vozes2 proposal, contributing to stronger, more organized, and impactful communication. The preparation carried out in May was essential to ensure that the event has a consistent visual identity and well-structured materials.

The artwork, budgeting, and production monitoring were done by the design team.
